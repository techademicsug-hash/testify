Unfailing Mercies for a Brand New Morning

"The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness." Lamentations 3:22-23 (ESV)

When Jeremiah penned these words, his homeland was in ruins and sorrow seemed triumphant. Yet from the deepest place of grief, the Prophet lifted his eyes to heaven and recalled the unchanging character of God. Yesterday's trials do not carry a monopoly on today.

Before your feet touched the floor, God had already dispensed fresh compassion tailored precisely for every burden you will meet today. His mercy is not recycled; it is freshly minted.

Guided Morning Prayer "Heavenly Father, thank You that Your faithfulness is deeper than my weakness. I receive Your fresh mercies for this day. Grant me peace in my mind, purity in my speech, and strength in my spirit to honor Christ in my home and workplace. In Jesus' name, Amen."
