Tuesday, October 13, 2026 By Pastor Thomas Vance

New Mercies for Weary Mornings

"The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness."
Lamentations 3:22-23

Jeremiah penned these timeless words in the deepest valley of his life. Jerusalem had fallen, the temple lay in ruins, and sorrow felt like a heavy shroud. Yet right in the heart of his lamentation, the prophet anchors his soul on an immutable truth: God’s compassions do not expire at sunset.

When you wake with unresolved burdens or anxious thoughts, remember that God does not expect you to run on yesterday’s exhausted strength. His mercies are a fresh spring, bubbling up anew with the sunrise.

Guided Prayer for Today

"Heavenly Father, thank You that Your faithfulness does not waver with my circumstances. Forgive me for holding onto yesterday’s anxiety. I receive Your fresh mercy for this day. Give me peace in my home and eyes to see Your gentle hand in every hour. In Jesus’ name, Amen."
